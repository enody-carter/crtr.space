I am Carter Peterson and this is my space on the internet.

I currently reside in Brooklyn, NY and spend my time running [Enody Lighting](https://enody.lighting). I believe lighting has a direct and significant impact on the physiological systems of all living organisms. The goal of Enody is to discover and interact with these systems.

Prior to my Enody, I worked at Apple Inc. on a handful of interesting projects. The most well known would be the Google Apple Exposure Notification system, of which I wrote the underlying advertisment storage and risk calculation system.
